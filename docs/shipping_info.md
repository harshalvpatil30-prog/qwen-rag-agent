# Shipping Information

- Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and costs 50 rupees.
- Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 150 rupees.
- Orders above 999 rupees get free standard shipping.
- We ship to all major cities in India. International shipping is not available yet.
- Orders placed before 2 PM are dispatched the same day.
- A tracking link is sent by SMS and email once the order is dispatched.
