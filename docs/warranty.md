# Warranty Terms

- All electronics come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty from the date of purchase.
- The warranty covers manufacturing defects only. It does not cover water damage, drops, or unauthorized repairs.
- To claim warranty, keep your invoice and contact support with the product serial number.
- Repairs take up to 10 business days. If a repair is not possible, we replace the product.
- Accessories such as cables and chargers have a 6-month warranty.
