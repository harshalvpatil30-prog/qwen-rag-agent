# Refund Policy

- Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method and take 5 to 7 business days to appear.
- Digital products are refundable only if they have not been downloaded.
- Items damaged in transit are eligible for a free replacement or a full refund.
- To request a refund, email support@examplestore.com with your order ID.
